🕌 Masjid Valley

A cozy game about bringing a masjid back to life — one Hijri year, five prayers a day. No faces, no chance, halal only.

Restoring a room visibly changes the masjid. The carpet is the first project — the minaret, wudu area, library, garden wall and dome come in the full game.
The masjid garden — 6 plots. Yields are fixed, never random: a well-watered crop always gives its full yield.

Tap a plot with the tool you need. Watered crops grow one stage each time you go to prayer (two with barakah). Crops dry out overnight, so water again each day.

The masjid cat
Feed it 1 crop of any kind. At 3 feeds it follows you around the courtyard — and once the new carpet is in, it sleeps on it.
You arrive at your grandmother's masjid. Imam Yusuf hands you the keys. "Clear the courtyard, fix the garden wall — start with the garden."
🌙 The courtyard is alive again!
Carpet restored, both neighbours helped, the cat fed and following you. That is the whole loop — work, earn, restore — in one sitting.
